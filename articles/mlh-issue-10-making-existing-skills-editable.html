What I learned and what I would carry forward

The early PR accumulated formatting-only changes that obscured the feature. A separate cleanup pass made the final route change easier to review. The issue was still open at the scan date, and the later linked PR #28 was also open.

The lasting lesson is specific to this record: Editing matters as much as adding: a resume builder has to support the way people's experience changes. The next time I meet a similar problem, I would start by writing down the observable behavior, the constraints that define a correct result, and the smallest check that would distinguish a real fix from a plausible-looking change. For a design project, that means making requirements and drawings traceable. For a code contribution, it means connecting the issue, patch, review response, commit, and test output so a maintainer can follow the reasoning without reconstructing it from memory.
