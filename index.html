What keeps the projects interesting?

The spark is the moment a real constraint creates a question we can test: a label has 63 characters, a conference visitor needs the right page on a phone, a workload spends time waiting, or a controller must distinguish success from failure. Those questions interest me because a small decision changes what a user, device, or service can actually do. The work starts as coursework, team responsibility, fellowship work, or curiosity; the story is what happened when the first design met evidence. Say hello if you want to compare notes.
